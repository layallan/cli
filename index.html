About the lab ↓

Welcome to the Center of Light Intelligence Research Lab

We develop Light Intelligence for scientific discovery and new applications in biosensing, quantum technology, and energy. We combine programmable light, physical models, and machine learning to design experiments around the questions we want to answer.

Meet the team →

Research directions

Light Intelligence

We learn which properties of light reveal the most about the system under study.

Biosensing, Precision Health and Biopharmaceutical Discovery

Optical sensing and imaging to characterize biological samples and support therapeutic discovery.

Light for Quantum Science, Energy & Climate

We generate quantum light and use ultrafast spectroscopy to study molecules and materials for energy conversion and sustainable chemistry.

Autonomous Science and Advanced Instruments

Digital twins and adaptive control for complex scientific instruments.

Education & opportunities

We prepare the next generation of scientists, engineers, and entrepreneurs for careers in academia, industry, and business. We welcome applicants from all backgrounds and especially encourage first-generation college students and members of groups underrepresented in science and engineering to apply.

Our projects span photonics, machine learning, and scientific instrumentation, with collaborators around the world. Tell us what you would like to investigate. We would be happy to discuss a project that fits your interests.
